# DD Distribution Ltd. – 2020 Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project involved analysing 2020 sales data for DD Distribution Ltd., a Nigerian FMCG distribution company. The aim was to examine sales performance across products, product categories, producers, customers and sales channels, and present the findings in an interactive Excel dashboard.

The analysis was designed to help management understand sales patterns, identify major revenue contributors and make more informed decisions about inventory, customer relationships and sales strategies.

## Business Objectives

The analysis focused on the following objectives:

- Evaluate overall sales performance.
- Identify the highest-performing products and product categories.
- Examine sales contributions by producer.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Compare performance across sales channels.
- Identify monthly sales trends.
- Present key findings through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data analysis, calculations and reporting.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **PivotTables:** Data aggregation and analysis.
- **PivotCharts:** Visual representation of sales findings.
- **Excel Slicers:** Interactive dashboard filtering.

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contained 434 product-order records covering January to December 2020.

It included information on:

- Order dates and order numbers
- Customer names and customer types
- Producers and product names
- Product categories and variants
- Quantities, unit prices and sales
- Delivery information
- Sales channels

An important distinction is that each row represents a product line within an order, not necessarily a separate order. Therefore, distinct counts were used when calculating the number of orders and customers.

## Data Preparation

The dataset was prepared for analysis using Excel and Power Query.

Key preparation activities included:

- Reviewing the available fields and data structure.
- Preparing date-related fields for monthly and other time-based analysis.
- Calculating sales using quantity multiplied by unit price.
- Organising the data for PivotTable analysis.
- Using distinct counts for order numbers and customer names to avoid counting repeated product lines as separate orders or customers.

## Analysis and Key Findings

The analysis produced the following results:

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | ₦8,084,704.50 |
| Total Orders | 171 |
| Total Customers | 104 |
| Average Order Value | ₦47,278.97 |
| Products | 30 |

### 1. Monthly Sales Performance

April recorded the highest monthly sales at ₦1,485,750, followed by February at ₦1,321,120. June recorded the lowest sales at ₦94,240.

This shows considerable variation in monthly sales performance and highlights the need to investigate the factors behind the differences.

### 2. Product and Category Performance

Nestle Milo was the highest-selling product, contributing ₦2,512,040, approximately 31.1% of total sales.

Chocolate Beverage and Milk were the leading categories, contributing approximately 36.2% and 32.7% of total sales respectively. Together, they accounted for approximately 68.9% of total sales.

### 3. Producer Performance

Nestle Nigeria Plc recorded the highest sales contribution at ₦3,360,852.50, representing approximately 41.6% of total sales. Arla Foods followed with ₦1,775,825.

### 4. Customer Analysis

General Trade accounted for approximately 83.8% of total sales.

Rodam Enterprises recorded the highest customer sales contribution at ₦2,107,960 and placed 19 distinct orders.

### 5. Sales Channel Performance

The Mobile App generated the highest sales at ₦3,879,192.50, followed by WhatsApp at ₦2,818,300 and USSD at ₦1,387,212.

### 6. Variant Analysis

Milo Sachet 20g x 240 recorded the highest quantity sold, with 175 units. Dano Cool Cow Sachet 12g x 260 + 30 Sachet Free followed with 171 units.

## Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following actions are recommended:

- Monitor stock levels and replenishment needs for high-performing products.
- Prioritise the availability of products within the leading sales categories.
- Strengthen relationships with frequent and high-value customers.
- Review the performance of sales channels and improve the customer ordering experience.
- Investigate the reasons behind monthly sales fluctuations, particularly the low sales recorded in June.

## Dashboard

The Excel dashboard presents the analysis through KPI cards, charts and interactive slicers. It allows users to explore sales performance across different dimensions, including month, producer, product category and sales channel.

## Limitations

- The dataset covers only 2020, so year-on-year comparisons could not be performed.
- Cost and expense information was unavailable; therefore, profitability could not be assessed.
- The dataset does not contain wider market information, so market share could not be determined.
- The analysis identifies patterns and associations but does not establish the causes of sales performance.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw sales records into meaningful business insights. Through data preparation, analysis and dashboard development, the project provides a clearer view of the company's sales performance and highlights areas that management can investigate when making business decisions.
